MK1300 V2 key repair (v2)

This version uses the correct key format, which I got wrong before. Working keys on your board look like 32, 0, KEYCODE, 0, while the broken ones look like 32, KEYCODE, 7, 0.

Step 1 (read only): click Connect and read keys.
Step 2: if broken keys are listed, tick the box and click Repair the wrong keys. It writes only the keys marked WRONG, on layer 0.

Use Chrome or Edge. Keep your laptop keyboard handy.
